Task 1: Student Eligibility & Grade System
Objective:

Build a Console Application that takes a student's details and determines their eligibility and academic grade based on their score and attendance rate.

User Inputs:

Student Name (string)

Exam Score out of 100 (int or double)

Attendance Rate out of 100% (int or double)

Business Logic & Requirements:

Attendance Check (Primary Condition):

If the attendance rate is below 75%, output:

"Status: Failed - Reason: Low Attendance Rate" (Do not evaluate the grade).

Grade Evaluation (If attendance is 75% or higher):

90 – 100: Grade: A (Excellent)

80 – 89: Grade: B (Very Good)

70 – 79: Grade: C (Good)

50 – 69: Grade: D (Pass)

Below 50: Grade: F (Fail)

Use int.Parse() or Convert.ToDouble() to handle input conversions.

Validate the inputs: If the user enters a score or attendance outside the range of 0 - 100, display an error message: "Invalid Input: Values must be between 0 and 100."
